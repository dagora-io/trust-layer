# 1 · Purpose

Work already has tools that write, run, and ship. What is usually missing is a durable record of *whether this step should be taken*, and a rule that the record cannot authorize the step by itself.

The Trust Layer is that record and that rule.

It is for people who have to decide under incomplete evidence. It is not a model ranking, not a coding assistant, and not a substitute for the person who still has to accept or refuse.

## Normative sentences

1. A recommendation is advice. Advice is not approval.
2. A receipt is evidence that advice was given. A receipt is not a pass.
3. A claim must name its grade. Ungraded certainty is non-conformant.
4. If the next action would be hard to reverse or would have effects outside the local workspace, the envelope must say so in `UNRESOLVED` or must refuse to recommend go.

## Out of scope

- Executing the recommended step
- Merging, deploying, billing, or changing production state
- Identity of any particular model or vendor
- Scoring products against each other

Those belong to other systems. This specification does not bind them.
